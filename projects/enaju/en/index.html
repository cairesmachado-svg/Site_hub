Applied institutional work

Education as an implementation infrastructure, not merely a catalogue of courses.

The agenda includes cooperation among judicial staff schools, professional learning initiatives, and the organization of evidence on training provision. The first national meeting of judicial staff schools took place on 11 November 2026. The CNJ reported three proposed priorities for 2027: national courses, shared educational content, and a collaborative network of judicial schools.
