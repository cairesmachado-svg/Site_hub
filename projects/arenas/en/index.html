RESEARCH QUESTION

How do sociomaterial arrangements shape what public policy becomes when it is implemented in justice institutions?

OVERVIEW

From architecture to implementation.

ARENAS extends the agenda that began with ARQJUD. The building is no longer observed in isolation but as part of networks of rules, people, technologies, documents, capacities, and practices.

Materiality

Spaces, systems, documents, and infrastructures are not background conditions. They participate in the possibilities and limits of public action.

Implementation

The focus is on how institutional decisions move through routines, local capacities, and concrete execution conditions.

Governance

The project connects coordination, regulation, institutional capacity, and evidence production in justice systems.

WORKSTREAMS

A layered research program.

The streams are complementary and may generate articles, indicators, protocols, and applied research products.

01

Foundations

Sociomateriality, public policy implementation, and judicial regulatory governance.

02

Empirical design

Cases, units of analysis, indicators, and data-collection strategies.

03

Measurement and explanation

Extending the ARQJUD experience toward instruments and models connecting sociomaterial conditions and implementation.
