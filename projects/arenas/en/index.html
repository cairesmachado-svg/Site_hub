Research question

How do sociomaterial arrangements shape what public policy becomes when it is implemented in justice institutions?

From architecture to implementation

ARENAS extends the agenda that began with ARQJUD. The building is observed as part of networks of rules, people, technologies, documents, capacities, and practices.

01

Materiality

Spaces, systems, documents, and infrastructures participate in the possibilities and limits of public action.

02

Implementation

How institutional decisions move through routines, local capacities, and concrete execution conditions.

03

Governance

Coordination, regulation, institutional capacity, and evidence production in justice systems.

Three workstreams

Complementary streams. Each may generate articles, indicators, protocols, and applied research products.

  1. 01

    Foundations

    Sociomateriality, public policy implementation, and judicial regulatory governance.

  2. 02

    Empirical design

    Cases, units of analysis, indicators, and data-collection strategies.

  3. 03

    Measurement and explanation

    Extending the ARQJUD experience toward instruments and models that connect sociomaterial conditions and implementation.
