PRINCIPLE

Use agents to expand research capacity without transferring the researcher’s epistemic responsibility to them.

WORKFLOW

From problem to product.

The method organizes agents, data, code, and writing into a workflow that can be verified, taught, and reproduced.

1 · Architect

Problem, question, protocol, eligibility, file structure, and agent roles.

2 · Execute

Search, APIs, extraction, cleaning, analysis, logs, and human checkpoints.

3 · Publish

Quarto, references, visualizations, artifacts, versioning, and reproducible documentation.

TEACHING CASE

Learn by building a real study.

The pedagogical proposal uses a complete research project as a case for a semester course with one three-hour meeting per week.

01

Problem and protocol

Turn a research question into a documented operational architecture.

02

Data and APIs

Scopus, structured sources, automation, and quality control.

03

Writing and product

From QMD to final outputs while preserving traceability from evidence to analysis and narrative.

OUTPUTS

Research and training in the same system.

The project is designed to generate both a scientific case and reusable teaching materials.

Next structural decision: create a dedicated public repository for the project to host the syllabus, protocol, case study, examples, and teaching materials.

Want to test or co-develop the workflow?

Participation is welcome from researchers, faculty, students, and teams interested in reproducible AI-assisted research.
