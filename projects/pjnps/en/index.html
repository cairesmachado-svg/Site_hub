Research question

How can national judicial policies be identified, organized, and assessed through a transparent empirical protocol rather than inferred from policy labels alone?

The program connects a bibliometric account of judicial public policy research with documentary decisions about legal acts, implementation mechanisms, and observable institutional evidence.

Methodological design

A reproducible workflow with explicit human checkpoints and auditable eligibility decisions.

01

Cartography

Identify conceptual domains and research trends through structured bibliometric evidence.

02

Documentary corpus

Define the population of judicial-policy instruments and preserve provenance from source to extraction.

03

Adjudication and analysis

Separate algorithmic assistance, coding suggestions, expert decisions, and validated findings.

Research status. Design decisions, network-dependent collection steps, and a pilot are pending. No complete national results are claimed here.
