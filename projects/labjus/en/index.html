PURPOSE

Create an environment where students can investigate legal problems using interdisciplinary methods and applied research.

AXES

Law in dialogue with other disciplines.

The laboratory design emphasizes real problems, collaboration, and verifiable outputs rather than an exclusively doctrinal approach.

Empirical legal research

Data collection and analysis applied to justice-system problems.

Behavior and decision-making

Heuristics, biases, institutions, and the design of decision environments.

Applied research

Projects capable of producing reports, diagnostics, prototypes, and research agendas.

CURRENT OUTPUT

Institutional presentation.

The existing repository page remains preserved as a specific artifact of the proposal.
