Original question

How does judicial architecture relate to workplace experience and self-perceived productivity in justice institutions?

Three factors for observing judicial space

The assessment is organized around three dimensions that connect spatial experience and institutional functioning.

01

Recognition

Identity, legibility, and perception of the institutional environment.

02

Access

Conditions of arrival, circulation, orientation, and use of justice spaces.

03

Functionality

How well the environment supports routines, flows, tasks, and workplace needs.

The project that opened the sociomaterial agenda

ARQJUD findings support the transition toward studying infrastructure as a component of public policy implementation.

    PhD research
