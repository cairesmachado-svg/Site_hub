Research

The research object is the passage from rules and institutional decisions to concrete practice. Implementation is the analytical core. Four dimensions organize the agenda.

  • ImplementationCore

    Where rules, capacities, spaces, technologies, and practice meet.

  • Judicial governance

    How do coordination, institutional capacities, and governance instruments shape implementation in justice systems?

  • Regulation

    How do intermediaries translate, certify, monitor, and transform rules into behavior?

  • Sociomateriality

    What do buildings, technologies, documents, and infrastructures do to policy in practice?

  • Judicial education

    How do education, knowledge, and learning expand or constrain institutional capacity?

Methods and AI. Bibliometrics, APIs, Quarto, reproducible workflows, and AI agents serve as research infrastructure for traceable evidence. See Agentic Scientific Research.
